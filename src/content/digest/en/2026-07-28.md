    summary: "Forbo has unveiled Marmoleum Cocoa, a flooring product that repurposes agricultural waste from cacao production in West Africa while supporting local communities. Composed of up to 98% recycled materials, the product demonstrates sophisticated CMF strategy by transforming agricultural byproducts into tactile, visually distinctive surfaces. The design exemplifies how material innovation can simultaneously address sustainability and socioeconomic impact."
